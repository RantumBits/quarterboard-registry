What the labels mean

Fully US-made Cut and sewn, or lasted and handsewn, in America. No asterisks.

US-made line The named line is made here. Other items from the brand are not. Check per item.

How we verify

We check the specific product page, not the homepage slogan. We look for where the item is cut and sewn and where the material comes from, since those can be different countries. When a brand goes vague, we email and ask.
